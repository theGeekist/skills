# Geekist Skills v1.9.0

Two independently installable outreach skills join the writing collection. They support informed professional conversations without fixed sales-pitch templates or scheduled-task dependencies.

## New skills

- **research-outreach-opportunities** investigates companies or people, consults connected CRM history when available, checks current first-party and independent evidence, challenges initial hypotheses, and preserves alternative possibilities. CRM history is authoritative for relationships, not necessarily for current company activities.
- **write-outreach** composes and revises one-to-one professional messages using actual relationship context, specific discoveries and the author's editorial judgement. It can work directly from a draft or conversation without mandatory research.

The task owns candidate selection, quotas, professional-evidence locations, CRM schemas and permitted writes, scheduling and delivery. Neither skill requires Google Drive, Lumos, WriteWithJ or a particular automation.

## Authorial learning

A consequential correction can update an authorised persistent voice reference with its original context and transferable reasoning. It does not turn into prohibited-word lists, synonym swaps or reusable sales templates.

The Hyperbeam case remains a research example of a changed commercial hypothesis, separate from individual approved correspondence.

## Protected outcomes

- The workflow may require an investigation to be completed. It cannot require the investigation to discover a sales opportunity.
- The workflow may require an outreach assessment. It cannot require that assessment to produce a message worth sending.

## Installation

```sh
npx skills add https://github.com/theGeekist/skills/tree/v1.9.0
```

Select the required skills and target agent. ChatGPT plugin registration remains a separate distribution process; native invocation depends on the client.
