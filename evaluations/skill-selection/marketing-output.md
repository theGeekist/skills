Subject: Meet Fieldnote: a notes beta for independent consultants

Your notes help you carry ideas from one consulting assignment to the next. Fieldnote is a desktop notes app for independent consultants, now available as a beta for individual trials.

Store notes on your device, search them by title and text, and export Markdown files so you can use your writing beyond the app.

Fieldnote is built for individual use: it has no shared workspaces, cloud backup or team collaboration. Encryption has not been implemented, so consider what you put into your trial notes.

Try the beta with a non-sensitive note and see how searching and Markdown export fit your working day.
