Subject: Meet Fieldnote: desktop notes for independent consultants

When you need to revisit an idea or find a detail from your notes, having searchable text matters. Fieldnote’s beta is now available for individual trials, giving independent consultants a desktop place to keep notes on their own device.

Search by title or text to find what you wrote, and export Markdown files to use your notes beyond the app.

This beta has no cloud backup, and encryption has not been implemented. For your trial, use non-sensitive notes and keep your own backups.

Try the beta with a set of non-sensitive notes to see how search and Markdown export fit your working day.
