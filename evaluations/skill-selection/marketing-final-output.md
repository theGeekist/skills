Subject: Meet Fieldnote: desktop notes for independent consultants

Hello,

Your notes should be easy to revisit when the next question comes up. Fieldnote’s beta is now available for independent consultants to try individually.

Keep notes on your device, search their titles and text, and export them as Markdown files for use beyond the app. It’s a straightforward way to keep your own working notes searchable and portable.

The beta has no cloud backup, and encryption has not been implemented, so use non-sensitive sample notes for your trial.

Try the beta with a few sample notes: search for a detail, then export them to see whether Fieldnote fits the way you work.
