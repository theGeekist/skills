# Drafting prompts

Use only the prompts that serve the reader. Replace them with descriptive headings in the final artefact.

## Task

- What outcome should change?
- What existing behaviour or constraint matters?
- Which concrete cases demonstrate success and failure?
- How will the work be verified?

## Decision record

- What is the decision and its status?
- What problem and constraints drove it?
- Which viable options were considered?
- What benefits, costs and consequences follow?
- Where is the supporting evidence?

## Architecture explanation

- What is this system or boundary for?
- What should a reader outside engineering understand first?
- What are the principal decisions and their effects?
- Which terms need a short explanation?
- Where can engineers inspect the mechanism, contracts and trade-offs?

## Product content

- What does the reader need to know or do?
- What result and movement can the data actually support?
- Which definitions must remain consistent with the domain?
- What evidence and next action help the reader?

## Conversation

- What answer or finding matters now?
- What supporting detail changes the reader's understanding?
- Does a new term need explanation or explicit proposal?
- What remains uncertain or needs a decision?
