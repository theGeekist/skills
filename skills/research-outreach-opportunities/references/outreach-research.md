# Outreach research — Sources and adapted principles

This reference supports the `research-outreach-opportunities` skill. It does not prescribe a prospecting score, a customer-needs template or a fixed investigation sequence.

## Research principles

The useful research outcome is a defensible point of view about a company or person. Start with existing relationships and records where available, then inspect current first-party sources and independent usage. Challenge the initial hypothesis when contrary evidence appears. Distinguish observed business activity, reasonable opportunity hypotheses and actual commissioned work.

Use the depth warranted by the discovery. The investigation can end with a commercially useful observation, an alternative approach or a recommendation not to contact anyone yet. Preserve important positive findings and source links rather than treating the absence of a deficiency as a failed lead.

CRM records establish known identities and correspondence history, but current primary and independent sources establish whether products, documentation, activity and opportunities have changed. A CRM finding can be superseded without rewriting or discarding the original historical research.

CRM records are first-party relationship context, not a fixed vendor schema. The invoking task determines candidate eligibility, authoritative document locations, persistence operations, quotas, statuses and message delivery. The skill determines how to reason about the opportunity and how to preserve the research when authorised.
